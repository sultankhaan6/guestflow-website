FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Straight answers to important questions.

Are my guests talking to AI or real people?

Real people. GuestFlow is built around human agents who understand context, tone, unusual requests and when something needs your attention. Technology may help behind the scenes, but your guests are not simply handed off to an AI chatbot.

So... what exactly does GuestFlow take off my plate?

Day-to-day guest messages, common questions, instructions, issue triage, follow-ups and selected remote operations — tailored to how you already run your business.

What types of rental businesses do you support?

GuestFlow is not limited to Airbnb. We can support short-term rentals, vacation rentals, car-rental hosts and other rental businesses where guest or customer communication matters.

What happens when something goes wrong?

We follow the process you approve in advance. Routine issues are handled by the team, while urgent or owner-level situations are escalated appropriately.

Can you follow the way I already run my business?

Yes. GuestFlow is designed to work around your processes, tone, rules and preferences instead of forcing you into a rigid system.
